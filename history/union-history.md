# Union History

## Working Conditions Before the Union

- Part-time employees did not have healthcare.
- Part-time employees did not always have good parking options and paid more than full-time employees.
- We did not have a grievance procedure, because employees could not grieve egregious management behavior.
- One director gave stipends to favorite employees for "special projects".
- Our only representation with management was through the Staff Association, which had no bargaining rights.

## 2023–2025

- Worked in the contract a request for dialogue to occur if staff layoffs were to occur.
- Part-time incentive increase to make it more accessible for part time staff to join the union.
- Training guidelines for LMC participation.

## 2020
### Post-Pandemic

- The union strongly influenced the decision to close the library in March.
- The union wrote the petition that paused bringing all staff back and got staff a seat at the decision making table.
- Union pressure and conversation with management got leadership to use commentable Google docs all staff could see and engage with.
- The union was able to work with leadership to provide some stress relieving options during the pandemic.

### Pre-Pandemic
 
- Rearranging the 2nd floor information desks to better fit the safety and needs of staff.
- The union was able to influence leadership to ask the city for more parking tags.
- The union negotiated for staff to have access to patron comments.
- The union helped shape the structure of Continuous Improvement.
- The union negotiated to get additional clarification about harassment added to the personnel manual.
- Explored the idea of having union meetings at the library outside of library hours. Currently this is prohibited by our Agreement, but agreed that we could explore this idea.

## 2019

- The union advocated hard for a security person to be at Ellettsville.
- Discussed concerns about leave requests in CoCu with the schedule being published four weeks in advance and leave requests responded to within one week.
- Worked on parking issues. Sent a letter to the City about our concerns.
- Discussed concerns about reference desk usability, patron bans, and other workplace environment issues.
- Reviewed Personnel Manual addition related to "pervasive and disrespectful treatment of others" in employee discipline and termination.
- Discussed library-wide concerns about communication within and between units and among strategists/managers in regards to staff needs, productivity, and accountability.
- Worked on language to include in the Personnel Manual for appropriate use of personal pronouns.
- Addressed issue with consistency with counseling as some staff are being told to work out issues directly with peers and manager may be abdicating responsibility.

## 2018
- Brought up concerns regarding the first aid kits not being stocked properly which prompted Brian to get them restocked.
- Asked for access to all patron comments. New categories where added to the comments so that they can be more easily searched and sorted and comments brought to the union when requested. Now all patron comments are summarized in the director's report.
- Asked about access to employee salaries that were previously reported in the HT. [All employee salaries are now available in the State database](https://gateway.ifionline.org/report_builder/Default3a.aspx?rptType=employComp&rpt=EmployComp&rptName=Employee%20Compensation).
- Asked about doing 360 evaluations of management.
- Discussed issues regarding homebound/van service scheduling because staff were not being given enough time to complete assigned tasks. Outreach schedules were updated to provide greater scoverage support.
- Updated policy so that emergency closures don't require branch staff to finish shifts at Downtown when that isn't actually helpful.
- Discussed whether significant changes to jobs or job descriptions should be brought to LMC.

## 2017
- Worked on updating coaching/counseling/discipline and grievance procedures.
- Discussed librarians at Ellettsville having to remain available during unpaid lunch breaks and getting an updated procedure there.
- Brought up concerns about the Ellettsville renovation regarding ADA compliance, parking, and safety.

## 2015
- Provided feedback from staff to administration about the New Service Model.
- Worked on getting an email account for the union to use to announce meetings and some other timely topics.
- Brought forward staff concerns about work location assignments. Some staff were being assigned to locations under the New Service Model that they were not familiar with working and did not request. Some assignments seemed to be given unfairly. The union pointed this out, repeatedly to administration.
- Asked for better communication about New Service Model changes.
- Worked to clarify expectations for staff that need to come in early to prepare for a program or stay late to ensure that they are not being reprimanded for working outside of their scheduled hours.

## 2012
- Worked on getting clarification on "duties as assigned", especially with the change that materials handlers are no longer allowed to wear headphones in the stacks.

## 2011
- Discussed fairness of additional hours for part-time staff during LMC to ensure all staff that want additional hours are afforded the opportunity to get additional hours when they are available.
- Worked on guidelines for discipline.
- Worked with management on changes for exempt and non-exempt staff timekeeping in preperation for expected Fair Labor Standards Act changes.
- Developed procedures to ensure that policy changes are discussed in LMC before they go to the Board of Trustees.

## 2010
- Management agreed to changes in the Personnel Manual regarding bereavement leave and then made those changes only for staff in the bargaining unit. Then management set new bereavement leave allowances for those outside the baraining unit. The union thought the baraining unit allowances were for all staff and we continued to fight until we got the same leave for all staff some years later.
