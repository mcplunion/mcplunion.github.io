# How to join?

Union eligibility encompasses both full-time and part-time staff members working at least 20 hours a week across all departments, excluding those in managerial (confidential) positions. Eligible staff may email [mcplunion@gmail.com](mailto:mcplunion@gmail.com) or turn in a completed Application for Membership form (green card) to any Union Officer or Steward to become a member. Application forms can be found pinned to Union bulletin boards in staff break rooms across all library branches. Interested staff should only apply for union membership on their own personal time.
