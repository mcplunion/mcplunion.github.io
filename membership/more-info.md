# How can I find out more?

Email [mcplunion@gmail.com](mailto:mcplunion@gmail.com) and our officers will be happy to discuss any questions or concerns you may have! You can also email us to be added to our communications list whether you are a member or not. We have union meetings typically once a month or once a quarter during quieter times, which we send information about through email. Members will receive meeting invitations via their personal emails.

Our union is committed to being inclusive and accessible to all MCPL staff, regardless of their membership status. Even if you're not a dues-paying member, we invite you to participate in union meetings to stay informed and engage in the union's efforts to represent MCPL staff. We value your input and believe that everyone should have a voice in shaping our workplace environment!

All communication regarding union business should only be done during staff personal time.
