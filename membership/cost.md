# How much does it cost?

Full time staff pay $21.49 and part-time staff pay $17.28 each pay period. This amount comes out every pay period directly from your paycheck—you don’t have to do anything. Dues help fund the greater AFSCME council, but also support our local, including social events like parties or picnics, retirement gifts, and most importantly, legal representation and negotiation efforts.

Part time members receive quarterly incentive checks to ensure equitable access to Union membership and its benefits.
